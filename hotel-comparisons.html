Compare hotel bookings on the whole stay

Make direct, portal and points bookings comparable before valuing the rewards or choosing the lowest headline rate.

Published 12 November 2026 · Aviosmaxxing practical guide

On this page

  2. Rebuild the basket before comparing points
  3. Verify loyalty eligibility for the booking channel
  4. Include the points alternative without ignoring cash charges
  5. Keep a booking decision you can recheck

A lower nightly rate is not always a lower cost for the stay you need. Build one comparison around the same dates, guests, room, inclusions and cancellation terms. Only then add rewards and benefits. Otherwise a “best rewards route” can simply be a different product.

Define one comparable stay

Write down the hotel, room category, number of guests, dates and total number of nights. Record the currency, payment date, final taxes and mandatory charges. Check breakfast, parking and any other item you would actually pay for. A nightly headline needs to become a final stay total before it belongs in the comparison.

Keep flexible and non-refundable prices separate. A flexible booking and a non-refundable booking are different choices even when they use the same room. If one costs £20 more, ask whether that flexibility is worth £20 to you. The points estimate does not answer that question.

Worked example: all hotel prices, reward values and inclusions below are hypothetical. They are not live hotel offers or statements of loyalty-programme eligibility.

Rebuild the basket before comparing points

Suppose two routes offer the same suitable room and cancellation terms. The direct room price is £510 with breakfast included. A portal advertises £480 without breakfast. Both require £20 of local charges. You intend to buy breakfast, which would add £32 to the portal booking.

Equivalent stay, including the breakfast you would buy
ItemDirectPortal
Room£510£480
Local charges£20£20
Breakfast added£0£32
Total cash cost£530£532
Estimated usable reward value£10£6
Estimated net cost£520£526

Direct wins by £6 under these invented assumptions. The portal's £30 cheaper room price did not survive a comparison of equivalent baskets. The £10 and £6 reward values are assumed usable values for this example, not published valuations of any programme.

If you would not buy breakfast, remove that £32 from the portal basket. Its cash cost becomes £500 and its estimated net cost £494. Do not subtract an additional breakfast “benefit” from the direct route: you have already made the cash baskets comparable. Counting a benefit twice is an easy way to manufacture a saving.

Verify loyalty eligibility for the booking channel

For the exact programme and rate, check whether the chosen booking earns points, qualifies towards status and receives the benefits you plan to use. Do not assume eligibility from the hotel brand or from entering a membership number. Conversely, do not write off a route without checking its actual conditions. The applicable rate and programme rules decide.

For a portal, check its separate merchant terms and whether changes or cancellation affect the proposed reward. Keep hotel points, portal points and Avios in distinct columns. If a transfer is part of the plan, establish its current conversion rules before combining them.

Include the points alternative without ignoring cash charges

A points booking belongs in the same basket. Record the required points, its cash charges, included items and cancellation conditions. Use a personal points valuation and compare it with the suitable cash option you would genuinely buy. The Avios value guide explains that calculation and why the most expensive cash fare is often the wrong benchmark.

If a reward night is unavailable for your dates or room needs, keep it out of the shortlist until you can actually book it. An attractive theoretical redemption does not provide accommodation for this trip.

Keep a booking decision you can recheck

Save the final quote, rate name, cancellation deadline with its stated time zone, included items, currency and sources. After a material change, recheck both the stay and rewards assumptions. Use Aviosmaxxing's hotel planner to prepare the question, then ask for a comparison of equivalent stays rather than a ranking by headline points.

Official places to check live terms

Read the selected hotel's own rate conditions and the relevant programme's current terms. For the programmes offered in this app, start with Marriott, IHG or ALL Accor, alongside the exact portal offer. This guide does not claim that a particular booking channel earns a particular benefit.
