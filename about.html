About Aviosmaxxing

A clearer way to compare rewards routes.

Aviosmaxxing helps you organise research about purchases, bookings and everyday spending. Its purpose is to make the cash price, rewards and conditions visible in the same decision.

Who operates the site

The operator is Gregory Tubby. For a correction or a question about this website, email tubbygreg@pm.me. The privacy notice explains data handling and privacy contacts.

Two kinds of information

The permanent guides explain comparison methods with fully worked hypothetical examples. They are readable without an account or an AI request. They do not maintain a live table of card rates, transfer offers or hotel eligibility rules.

The app prepares personalised research using your selected cards and programmes. Search here uses Puter and the visitor's allowance; Use your AI prepares a prompt for you to send to a provider. An answer is an AI-generated research suggestion. A displayed source or AI-reported checking date does not independently verify its claims.

Our comparison method

  1. Start with the cheapest suitable alternative, including delivery, mandatory charges and the conditions you need.
  2. Separate card rewards, portal rewards and merchant loyalty. Check each layer's eligible spending base and exclusions.
  3. Keep points currencies distinct until a usable conversion is confirmed.
  4. Show the cash required today as well as any estimated future reward value.
  5. Recalculate without uncertain rewards and retain the assumptions needed to recheck the decision.

Guides label invented figures as examples. They do not present those figures as current issuer terms. When applying a method to real spending, use current primary sources: the exact issuer product, merchant offer, programme conditions and final booking quote.

Sources, dates and corrections

A guide's publication date is the date that page was published, not a claim that every external offer was checked that day. Reference links point to official providers where readers can check live conditions. We do not describe an illustrative rate as available just because a provider's home page is linked.

Report a suspected error with the guide URL, the statement concerned and a supporting primary source. We can correct the explanation or remove an unsupported statement. Do not send passwords, card numbers or account access codes. Material updates should be identified with the affected guide so readers can recheck saved assumptions.

Advertising and recommendations

The site uses the supplied Google display unit only on the full guide pages, after the article. The chat screen, guide listing, About page and privacy notice do not load advertising. An advertisement is labelled and has a Hide ad control. It is separate from the guide's calculations and from personalised AI responses. Google's privacy choices and the browser's advertising block still control whether an eligible placement loads.

These guides do not contain affiliate tracking links. No payment, commission or partnership with a rewards provider is asserted by mentioning it. Aviosmaxxing is an independently operated website and is not British Airways, a card issuer or a loyalty programme.

Use the tools with your own decision in mind

The calculator uses the rates and eligibility you confirm. It cannot decide whether borrowing, a card fee or a trip is appropriate for you. A reward estimate does not reduce a bill due today. Start with the spending you intended to make and a budget you can afford, then use the comparison to choose between suitable routes.
