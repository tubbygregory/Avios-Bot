Privacy notice

Last updated: 18 October 2026. This notice covers aviosmaxxing.co.uk.

Aviosmaxxing is operated by Gregory Tubby, the data controller for this website. For privacy questions or requests, email tubbygreg@pm.me.

What stays on your device

Your selected cards and loyalty programmes, AI mode/provider and appearance preference are stored in your browser so the app can remember your choices. Example-question rotation uses session storage. Saved searches, answers and reward snapshots are stored locally only when you explicitly save them. The advertising-block preference is stored to remember your privacy choice.

These functional settings are separate from advertising consent. They remain until you change them or clear browser storage; session storage ends with the browser session. Use Tools → Saved to export or delete saved items. New chat clears the current conversation from the app’s memory; it does not delete saved items or data already sent to another service. Local items do not automatically sync between devices.

AI searches and external assistants

Search here uses Puter. Loading its SDK connects your browser to Puter. When you submit a search, your question, selected rewards and recent conversation are sent through Puter to the selected model provider. Your Puter account and allowance are handled by Puter. See Puter’s privacy policy.

Use your AI prepares a prompt and attempts to copy it to your clipboard. Opening ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini connects you to that provider; the prompt is sent when you paste and submit it yourself. That provider’s privacy policy and account settings apply. Aviosmaxxing cannot read your account or retrieve its answer automatically.

Do not include payment-card numbers, passwords or other sensitive information in searches or saved answers. Export files may contain personal search details; keep them private.

Google advertising and consent

We use Google AdSense to support the site. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as your IP address, device/browser information, page context, ad interactions and identifiers. Depending on your choices and applicable rules, cookies or similar storage may support advertising, personalisation, measurement and fraud prevention.

Google’s certified consent manager presents advertising choices where required, including in the UK, EEA and Switzerland. You can accept, reject or manage those choices. The app waits for the consent manager before loading the AdSense advertising code; missing or failed consent information keeps that code off. Where GDPR consent applies, this app also requires permission for device storage and Google’s vendor before loading it. Google remains responsible for applying all other advertising-purpose and regional signals.

You can reopen Google’s choices using Privacy choices → Review Google privacy choices. You can also enable Block advertising on this browser, regardless of region. This extra block does not erase Google’s existing cookies; review its choices or use your browser controls to manage those. Withdrawing consent or enabling the block reloads a page that has already loaded advertising, so that Google’s code does not continue running on that page. Your rewards and saved items are kept.

Google manages its own consent records, advertising data retention and any international transfers under its published policies. See how Google uses information from sites using its services and Google’s privacy policy. We have not added a separate analytics service.

Hosting and links

GitHub Pages hosts this site. Hosting and domain services receive technical request information needed to deliver it; their own policies apply. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Following an external source or issuer link connects you to that website. Third-party scripts, including Puter and Google, run in this page and are trusted service code.

Your rights and requests

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection rights include access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection. You can withdraw advertising consent at any time without changing the lawfulness of earlier processing. Contact the operator for requests relating to this site; for information held in a Puter or other provider account, use that provider’s privacy channels too. The site has no user account or server copy of your locally saved items.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. We update this notice when the site’s data use changes.
