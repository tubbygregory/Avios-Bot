Check a rewards recommendation before paying

Turn an AI answer into a verifiable decision: check the exact product, earning units, exclusions, evidence and final price.

Published 12 November 2026 · Aviosmaxxing practical guide

Aviosmaxxing can prepare a personalised research question and organise an answer. The last step is to check the claims that would change your spending. A confident answer, a source link or a displayed “date checked” does not by itself establish that an offer applies to you.

Start with a precise brief

Name the exact product or booking, delivery location, dates where relevant, budget and currency. Select only cards and programmes you actually hold. For a plan-based product, include your current plan. A recommendation based on a different card variant or membership is a different calculation.

For a purchase, include delivery and the return conditions you need. For travel, include the room or fare conditions, guests, baggage and flexibility that matter. Use Plan a search in the app to prepare these details, then read the question before sending it. The prepared text is editable.

Match each material claim to its own evidence

A source for a card's standard earning rules does not also verify a portal bonus. A merchant's generic home page does not establish eligibility for a particular promotional code. Build a small evidence record for each layer:

  • Payment: exact product, eligible spending base, points unit, rate, rounding, caps and excluded transactions.
  • Portal: merchant-specific offer, qualifying item and customer type, tracking instructions, permitted codes, exclusions and end date.
  • Loyalty or transfer: relevant membership, booking channel, earning unit, current conversion, minimums and any timing restrictions.
  • Price: final comparable basket, currency, delivery or mandatory charges, cancellation conditions and payment timing.

Open the primary source and check the relevant text rather than stopping at a search-result summary. If an answer links to an unavailable page or a source that does not support the claim, mark that layer unverified. Ask for the exact current terms or compare without the uncertain reward.

Catch a units mistake before it becomes a total

Invented example: these numbers illustrate arithmetic only. The fictional programme and conversion are not Membership Rewards, RevPoints or any real issuer offer.

Imagine an order has a £100 item and £10 delivery. Verified rules in this fictional example say the card earns one bank point per pound on £110, while a portal earns two Avios per pound on the £100 item only. The result is 110 bank points plus 200 Avios, not 310 Avios.

If the fictional bank programme also has a confirmed conversion of two bank points to one Avios, the card portion would become 55 Avios and the combined estimate 255 Avios. Without a usable, confirmed conversion, keep the two balances separate. The app's calculator follows this separation instead of silently treating all points as Avios.

Use dates as an audit trail

Record when you checked the source yourself and, where shown, the offer's end date and time zone. A current date printed by an AI model is not evidence that it opened the page. A source may also be current but refer to a different product or a targeted offer you cannot access.

The app marks comparison dates as AI-reported and the calculator asks you to confirm rates. Those controls help you keep assumptions visible; they cannot independently certify the terms. Recheck at checkout when price, eligibility or an offer deadline could change the decision.

Compare a conservative result too

Calculate the route once with every verified reward and again without any uncertain bonus. Compare both with the cheapest suitable alternative and include its own rewards. If the advantage disappears without one fragile assumption, decide whether the extra cash or effort is worth that uncertainty.

Do not enter card numbers, passwords or account access codes into a research question. The Use your AI mode prepares text for you to copy and send; it cannot read a provider account or verify the answer you later paste back. Saved recommendations stay in your browser unless you export or share them.

Keep the decision useful after checkout

Save the relevant terms and order evidence for your own records. Treat a pending reward as pending, and use the programme's stated process for any query. When researching again, check current terms rather than assuming the saved answer is still eligible. Primary sources for real product details

Use your exact issuer and programme: American Express Membership Rewards, Barclaycard Avios, Revolut RevPoints and the merchant's own published terms. These are starting points for checking live rules, not evidence that the fictional rates above are available.
